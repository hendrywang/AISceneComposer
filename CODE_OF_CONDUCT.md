# Code of Conduct

## Our Standards

This project should stay practical, respectful, and focused on the work.

Expected behavior:

- Be direct without being hostile.
- Critique code, designs, and tradeoffs rather than people.
- Assume contributors may have different native languages and experience levels.
- Keep discussions relevant to the project.
- Respect licensing, attribution, and security boundaries.

Unacceptable behavior:

- Harassment, threats, or personal attacks.
- Sexualized language or imagery in project spaces.
- Discriminatory remarks or exclusionary behavior.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Submitting assets without the right to redistribute them.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove comments, close issues, reject contributions, or
block participants whose behavior is harmful to the project community.

Report concerns privately to `hendry@innofun.digital`.
